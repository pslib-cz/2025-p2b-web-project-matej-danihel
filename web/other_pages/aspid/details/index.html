Aspid (Cloverlands)

The Aspid, also known as the Cloverlands, was an area cut somewhere around 2022. It was a vibrant place, full of clovers. Later, it was reworked into Verdania, presumably for the same reason as the Red Coral Gorge. Unlike the Red Coral Gorge, however, Verdania retains its beauty, though only in the form of a memory.

It had one subarea, known as Greenhaven, or Grovehome, the map of which is showcased in the gallery, though no gameplay footage of it remains.
The cut enemies and mechanics from this area are quite interesting. First, there was far more focus on "Aspid" enemies. These would have acid attacks, which would have drained Hornet's Silk. Surprisingly, some of these attacks still appear in the code, though they are disabled in the game.
