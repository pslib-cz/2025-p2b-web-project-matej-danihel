The Hollow Knight: Silksong went through many iterations of its content before its eventual release in 2025. This page showcases the removed and reworked enemies, areas, and mechanics.





Coral Gorge description

The Red Coral Gorge was an area in the west side of Pharloom. It went through different iterations, first being conceived in 2019 and finally being reworked into the Sands of Karak in 2021.

It is separated into 2 or 3 main areas; the Coral Steps, the Coral River, and Voltnest.
